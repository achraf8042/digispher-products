How a page gets easier to quote

A writing example: a direct answer versus a passage with nothing an AI can lift.

Direct answer an AI can lift
Question Heading: "What is the best payroll software for a 15-person team?"

"For a business with under 20 employees, choose payroll software that handles tax filing automatically and charges per employee. Expect to set it up in a day."

Why it works: Answers the heading's question in the very first sentence. Names specific nouns and constraints so it can be extracted without page context.
Passage with nothing specific to quote
Generic Heading: "Payroll Software Overview & Benefits"

"When it comes to choosing payroll software, there are many factors to consider, and every business is different. In today's fast-paced digital world, finding the right platform is critical. In this article we will explore several tips..."

Why it fails: Zero extractable facts. Fluffy filler text. The model cannot quote this without hallucinating specifics.

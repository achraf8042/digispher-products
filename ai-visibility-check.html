Fix 02

Bing Webmaster & IndexNow

Copilot and ChatGPT search rely heavily on Bing's live index. Connect your domain to Bing Webmaster Tools and enable IndexNow for instant indexing.

Fast-tracks retrieval for ChatGPT live browsing.
Fix 03

Organization Schema

Give LLMs an unambiguous machine-readable entity declaration with Schema.org JSON-LD and sameAs links to Wikidata and Crunchbase.

Bridges your brand entity to existing knowledge graphs.
Fix 04

BLUF Summaries (Bottom Line Up Front)

AI engines parse and chunk text in semantic blocks. Put the direct answer in the first 40–60 words of each section instead of burying it under narrative fluff.

Enables automated synthesis and extraction without confusing the RAG parser.
Fix 05

One Consistent Brand Description

If your homepage says "all-in-one AI platform", your LinkedIn says "creative boutique", and your footer says "marketing consultancy", LLMs hallucinate or skip you.

Standardize one 25-word canonical statement everywhere.

Skip the Myths: 3 Claims Checked

Don't waste engineering cycles chasing unverified hacks. Here is what official search engine documentation actually says.

Myth 1: Google-Extended

"Blocking Google-Extended removes you from AI Overviews."

The Reality: Google-Extended is only used for training Gemini and Vertex AI foundation models. Google AI Overviews use the standard Googlebot crawler. Blocking Googlebot removes you from Google Search entirely.

Source: Google Search Central Documentation
Myth 2: llms.txt

"Adding an llms.txt file is all you need for AI SEO."

The Reality: While helpful for LLM developer tools and markdown readers, major AI search engines do not currently use llms.txt as a replacement for standard HTML indexing and schema.

Source: OpenAI & Perplexity Crawler Protocols
Myth 3: FAQ Schema

"FAQ schema guarantees citations in AI answers."

The Reality: FAQ markup alone does not establish topical authority or semantic confidence. If the content lacks entity co-occurrence and third-party citation consensus, LLMs will synthesize competitors instead.

Source: Stanford GEO Research Benchmark

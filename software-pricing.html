The Silent Killer of Software

Most Software Startups Don't Die From Bad Code.
They Die From Broken Pricing Economics.

Picking arbitrary numbers out of thin air, underpricing to "get early traction", or launching agentic AI features without usage safeguards will drain your bank account before you reach product-market fit.

The AI Token Cost Vampire

One heavy user running autonomous agents or multi-step tool calls can burn through $80 in OpenAI or Anthropic API tokens while only paying you a flat $19/mo subscription. Uncontrolled variable costs destroy your business.

The Underpricing Curse

Charging $9/mo for an app that saves a business 10 hours a week attracts demanding customers who consume massive support resources while leaving you without enough margin to hire help or invest in growth.

The Calculation Paralyzer

When your pricing page has 14 complex feature checkmarks and arbitrary gates, buyers get confused and leave. A pricing page is a sales conversation compressed into a table—if they must calculate it, they won't buy.
